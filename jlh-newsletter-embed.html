A Legacy That Echoes

From a Record Store in Sweden
to the Oscars Stage

At this year's Academy Awards, composer Ludwig Göransson accepted his Oscar for the film Sinners and shared a story that stopped the room. His father once bought a John Lee Hooker album in a small Swedish record store. That album became what Göransson called the original spark that led him to pick up a guitar — and eventually become the world-class composer he is today.

"My granddaddy's sound didn't just define American music. It's still inspiring the next generation of creators, from Nashville to Stockholm and beyond."

— Glenn Thomas, Grandson of John Lee Hooker

Named for the
Gibson ES-335

The Guitar He Always Came Back To

8-Year Single Barrel  ·  Cask Strength  ·  Kentucky Straight Bourbon

John Lee Hooker loved experimenting with different sounds, but his heart always returned to the ES-335 and its true, pure, heartfelt blues. This bourbon embodies that same homecoming — warm and familiar on the approach, with an electrifying complexity that unfolds slowly, like a twelve-bar verse you never want to end.
